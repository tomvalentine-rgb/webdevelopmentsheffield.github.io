apprentago.co.uk

Website Design, Development and Maintenance for a startup

Read more about how we helped an early stage start-up get off the ground, and how we continue to support them as they grow to become a nationally recognised early careers platform.

Brief

What began as a relatively simple website designed to help young people discover apprenticeship opportunities has developed into a much broader careers platform supporting students, schools, colleges, employers and regional government. Over the years, our work on Apprentago.co.uk has therefore gone far beyond simply designing a website. The project has involved continuous web design, development, new functionality, maintenance, performance improvements and security work as the organisation and its audience have grown.

Background

Apprentago was created after its founder experienced first-hand how difficult it could be to find apprenticeship opportunities online. The original goal was simple: make apprenticeships easier to discover. As Apprentago grew, however, the website had to grow with it. The platform expanded from primarily helping students find opportunities into an ecosystem connecting several different audiences: Young people searching for apprenticeships and work experience Schools and colleges supporting students into employment Employers looking to reach and recruit young talent Mayoral Strategic Authorities and government-backed programmes supporting regional employment and skills initiatives This created an interesting web development challenge. The website needed to remain extremely simple for a 16 or 17-year-old visiting for the first time, while also communicating enough credibility and technical capability for employers, schools and government organisations evaluating Apprentago as a potential partner. That balance has shaped almost every stage of the site's development.

Web Design

The visual identity of Apprentago.co.uk has evolved considerably over the years.

As the organisation moved from an early-stage startup towards a recognised national careers platform, the website needed to feel increasingly established without becoming overly corporate.

A major focus has therefore been simplifying the user experience.

Pages have been redesigned around clearer typography, stronger hierarchy and much more obvious calls to action.

Instead of presenting visitors with large amounts of information immediately, the site increasingly focuses on answering three questions:

What is Apprentago?

Who is it for?

What can I do here?

Web Development

Behind the design has been a considerable amount of development work.

Apprentago.co.uk has gradually evolved from a marketing website into the front door of a much larger technology platform.

Development work has included creating and improving pages for:

  • Apprenticeship discovery
  • Work experience opportunities
  • School and college services
  • Employer recruitment services
  • Resources and careers content

The website also has to interact naturally with the wider Apprentago ecosystem.

While the marketing website introduces the platform, users can move into dedicated student, educator and employer products where considerably more complex functionality takes place.

As Apprentago expanded, the website architecture also needed to accommodate increasing amounts of content without making navigation unnecessarily complicated.

Reusable components, structured page layouts and clearer navigation were introduced so new services could be added without continually rebuilding the website from scratch.

Maintenance

Maintaining Apprentago.co.uk has been just as important as building new features.

As traffic increased, regular maintenance became necessary across the website's CMS, plugins, content, integrations and front-end code.

This included:

  • Updating website components and dependencies
  • Reviewing broken or outdated pages
  • Monitoring forms and links
  • Maintaining redirects
  • Improving responsive behaviour
  • Updating employer and platform content
  • Managing the content management system
  • Reviewing indexing and search engine visibility
  • Resolving compatibility issues following updates
  • Regularly backing up and monitoring the website

There have also been occasions where changes to the underlying website or third-party software caused sections of the site to display incorrectly.

Those issues were investigated and resolved quickly while additional monitoring was introduced to reduce the likelihood of similar problems affecting users.

For a platform used by large numbers of young people, maintenance is not simply about keeping the website looking good. Reliability matters just as much as design.

Security

As the profile and traffic of Apprentago increased, website security naturally became more important.

At one stage we identified a security-related issue affecting the WordPress website which resulted in parts of the site temporarily appearing corrupted.

The problem was investigated and resolved, with the website restored to normal operation.

Following the incident, additional monitoring and security tooling, including MalCare, was used to help monitor the WordPress installation and provide additional protection.

It also reinforced an important principle that has shaped the site's ongoing development: security cannot be treated as a one-off task.

WordPress installations, plugins and third-party services all change over time, so maintaining the site requires regular updates, monitoring, backups and reviewing anything unusual as soon as it appears.

The incident ultimately resulted in a more robust approach to maintaining Apprentago.co.uk as the platform continued to grow.

Performance

Performance has also become increasingly important as Apprentago has scaled.

A careers website needs to work well across very different devices and internet connections.

A student opening Apprentago on an older mobile phone should still be able to understand the service and find an opportunity quickly.

Performance work has therefore focused on reducing unnecessary complexity throughout the front end.

Images and visual assets have been reviewed to prevent oversized files slowing pages down.

Layouts have been simplified.

Unnecessary elements have been removed.

Responsive behaviour has been improved.

The structure of key landing pages has also been refined so that the most important information appears quickly rather than forcing users to load or navigate through unnecessary content.
