Can Social Media Replace a Website?

Published 5 June 2026

Can Social Media Replace a Website?

If you own a business, you've probably asked yourself this question at some point.

With Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and TikTok allowing businesses to promote themselves for free, it's easy to see why some people wonder whether a website is even necessary anymore.

After all, if customers can message you through social media, see your latest work, and keep up with your business updates, why spend time and money on a website?

The reality is that social media is a fantastic marketing tool, but it works best when it's supporting a website rather than replacing one.

Why Businesses Rely on Social Media

For many businesses, social media is the first step towards building an online presence.

It's free to get started, simple to use, and allows businesses to connect with customers almost immediately. Whether you're posting photos of completed work, sharing customer reviews, or promoting a special offer, social media makes it easy to get your business in front of people.

Many business owners are already comfortable using platforms like Facebook and Instagram, which makes them feel far less intimidating than building a website from scratch.

Because of this, some businesses end up putting all of their attention into social media and never create a website at all.

The Problem With Relying Only on Social Media

The biggest issue is simple: you don't actually own your social media accounts.

Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and TikTok all decide how their platforms operate. Algorithms change, features get updated, and the number of people seeing your posts can rise or fall overnight.

In extreme cases, accounts can even be restricted or removed.

If your business relies entirely on one platform, you're putting your online presence in someone else's hands.

Think of social media as rented space. It can be incredibly useful, but ultimately you're still playing by somebody else's rules.

Why A Website Still Matters

A website gives your business something social media can't: a place that belongs entirely to you.

It's where customers can find everything they need to know about your business in one place. Your services, contact details, testimonials and previous work can all be displayed exactly how you want them to be.

A professional website also helps build trust.

Think about your own behaviour. If you search for a business online and can't find a website, there's a good chance you'll question how established that business really is.

For many customers, a website is still one of the first things they look for before making contact.

Can Customers Find Your Business Without a Website?

Many customers begin their search for products and services through Google.

While social media profiles can appear in search results, a professional website gives businesses a much greater opportunity to rank for relevant search terms and attract potential customers.

A business website also allows you to:

• Provide detailed information about your services

• Answer common customer questions

• Showcase testimonials and previous work

• Improve visibility on search engines over time

For businesses looking to grow online, a website can become one of the most valuable digital assets they own.

Social Media And Websites Work Best Together

The good news is that you don't have to choose one or the other.

In fact, the strongest businesses use both.

Social media is excellent for getting attention. It helps you stay visible, engage with customers, and share regular updates about your business.

Your website is where those interested customers go next.

Someone might discover your business through an Instagram post, but visit your website to learn more about your services, read reviews, and make an enquiry.

Social media attracts attention.

Your website turns that attention into action.
