Our Web Design Process

From first conversation
to live website

Every project is different, but the process remains clear, collaborative
and focused on delivering the best result for your business.

01
Discovery Call

We start with a free consultation to understand your business in depth, including your products or services, your customers, your goals and what you need your website to achieve. From there, we review your existing online presence, including any existing website you may have or social media presence , before looking at your competitors and the wider industry landscape. By the end of the consultation, we'll have a clear understanding of your requirements, priorities and business goals, giving us everything we need to put together an accurate proposal and quote.

02
Proposal & Quote

Using everything we learned during the consultation, we put together a custom proposal based on your requirements, priorities and business goals. This sets out the approach we recommend for your website, including the design, development and SEO considerations needed to support how your business actually operates, rather than simply creating a site that looks good. We then provide a clear, fixed-price quote based on the time, complexity and resources required for your project, outlining exactly what is included, what it will cost and the expected delivery timescale, with no hidden fees.

03
Web Design

Now we understand your business and your goals we curate multiple website design options to build an online identity that makes your business look credible, trustworthy and professional. We offer unlimited design iterations for all our work to ensure your 100% happy with the site aesthetics before we move to the development phase.

04
Web Development

Once you are happy with the designs, we get to work turning them into a great business website that meets your business goals, is easy to use and loads quickly. We work across a wide range of web development solutions, from WordPress websites to fully bespoke builds and will choose the best solution to meet your preferences.

05
Ongoing website maintenance and support

Whilst there is no obligation to continue working with us once your site is live, many of our clients choose some level of website maintenance . This is tailored depending on the complexity of your website and your business goals, but ensures your website stays up to date stays up to date , secure and performing well.

Ask a Question
01 What is the difference between web design and web development? +

Web development is the technical, behind-the-scenes process of building a website using programming, coding, and data management. While web design focuses purely on the visual look, feel, and user experience, web development ensures the site actually functions. It powers critical elements like contact forms, e-commerce checkouts, and custom blog layouts so your site works exactly as intended.

02 What areas do you serve? Can I hire you if I’m outside Sheffield? +

While were based in Sheffield, we work with businesses all across the globe.

03 How long does it take to build a custom website from scratch? +

The time it takes to build a website all depends on the type of website you require. For a simple one-page website our team can normally have a first version live within a few days. For more complex websites with more complex features, it normally takes a couple of weeks. We provide a definitive timeline before any development begins but remain flexible should any requirements change as the project develops.

04 How much does a website cost? +

Because every project is unique, we provide tailored quotes based entirely on your specific goals and requirements. However, to see a rough breakdown of custom web development costs, please take a look at our guide to our pricing. We believe in honest partnerships, which is why we always provide clear and transparent pricing before any development begins, ensuring you get maximum value with absolutely no hidden surprises.

05 Do you build websites using WordPress, or do you custom code? +

We specialize in lightweight, high-performance web development . Depending on your business goals, we either build completely bespoke custom-coded websites or develop highly customized, lightweight WordPress sites. We never use heavy, bloated themes or excessive plugins, ensuring your site remains secure, fast, and easy to update.

06 How do you ensure my new website is optimised for Google rankings? +

Every website we build is engineered with technical SEO at its core. Google prioritizes fast, responsive, and well-structured websites, which is why we completely avoid heavy themes and bloated plugins. We optimize your site's code, structure, images, and mobile performance to hit top marks on Google’s Core Web Vitals. While achieving a top spot on page one requires an ongoing content and keyword strategy, we ensure your site has the flawless technical foundation needed to outrank your competition right out of the gate.

07 Can I update the content on my website myself once it’s live? +

Yes. Our sites are custom-built specifically for you and your team ensuring that you have full control of whatever you need. We ensure everyone is up to speed before we fully hand over the project, and always remain available if any assistance is required.

08 Do you provide ongoing website maintenance and SEO support? +

Yes, a website shouldn't just sit there after launch. We offer ongoing website maintenance, enhancements, security updates, and monthly SEO packages to ensure your site continues to climb the Google rankings, drive organic traffic, and convert visitors into customers long after going live.
