Straight answers on deposits, value and how we quote website projects.

Ask a Question
01 How much do I pay upfront? +

We ask for a 20% deposit to begin. The remaining payments are invoiced after the design phase and again when the site launches, so you are not funding the whole project before any work is done.

02 Why not choose a cheaper template website? +

Cheap template sites often look generic, load slowly and are hard to grow. We do not speed up delivery by relying on bloated themes. Every site is planned around your services, customers and the actions you want visitors to take.

03 Are these prices fixed? +

The figures on this page are a guide. Once we understand your requirements we send a fixed-price quote covering exactly what is included. If the scope changes later, we agree that before any extra cost is added.

04 How are payments made? +

Invoices are issued at each agreed milestone. Most projects follow the 20% / 40% / 40% split. You only pay for a stage after the work for that stage has been completed and approved.

05 Do you offer website maintenance after launch? +

Yes. Growth and Bespoke packages include an initial support period. After that we can continue with a website maintenance plan covering updates, backups and small content changes.
